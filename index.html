Who We Are
A one-person studio with a big idea

Technology should feel human. That's the core belief behind everything we build at YSRajput Studio.

YSRajput Studio is an independent digital studio focused on building apps and platforms that solve real everyday problems — for people everywhere.

We combine modern technology, AI-assisted development, and human-centered design to create products people actually want to use. Every project starts with a genuine problem, not a trend.

No large team, no VC funding — just focused, intentional work. Our small size is our strength: it means faster decisions, tighter quality, and products built with real care.

Our Approach
How we think about building

Three principles that guide every product decision at YSRajput Studio.

Problem-first, always

Every product starts with a real friction point that real people face. We don't build features for the sake of it — only what genuinely helps.

Mobile as the primary canvas

Our users live on their phones. Every UI, every interaction, every flow is designed mobile-first — not adapted from a desktop version as an afterthought.

Sustainable indie development

We build things that last, not things that burn out. Small team, clear focus, and a long-term view on every product we put into the world.

Let's build something meaningful
