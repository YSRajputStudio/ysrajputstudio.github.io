About This App

Just One Answer is a lightweight, fun, and intuitive decision-making tool that helps you get instant Yes or No answers to any question. Whether you're facing a tough decision or just want to have some fun, this app offers a simple and satisfying way to choose.

With a 75% accuracy rate (users say it's surprisingly right!) — sometimes, the best decisions come from trusting your instincts... or letting the universe decide.
